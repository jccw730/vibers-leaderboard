How scoring works

The table ranks season points. A night's judging score only decides who finishes where that night.

Each night

Judges score every build out of 30. That judging score sets the finishing place for the night, and the place earns league points:

  • 1st place10
  • 2nd place8
  • 3rd place6
  • 4th place4
  • Night win bonus (1st)+1
  • People's Choice (crowd vote)+1
  • Yellow card, each−2

Best possible night: 12 points (win plus People's Choice). The judging score itself is never added to the table.

The season

Season points (Pts) are the total of every night's league points. Nights counts nights on the card, Wins counts nights finished 1st. Best night and Last night on a viber card are single-night league points, not season totals. Example: 12 + 11 + 8 = 31 over 3 nights.

Ties

Vibers level on season points share the place, and the next place is skipped (two on 3rd, then 5th). If a tie decides the season title, it goes to more wins, then the higher best night, then more People's Choice awards.

Movement

Arrows compare each viber's place now with the standings before the latest night (season points minus last night's points, ranked the same way). NEW means the viber's first points came on the latest night.
